Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026. Draft: have a lawyer review before public launch.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, name, username, and an optional bio.
  • What you post: photos, captions, comments and likes.
  • Reports: reports you file about posts.
  • Technical data: basic information such as your device, browser and IP address, which our hosting and database providers record for security.

We do not ask for caste certificates, phone numbers, addresses or government ID numbers. Please do not post them.

2. Why we use it

To run your account, show your posts to other members, keep the community safe, review reports, and fix problems. We do not sell your data.

3. Who can see it

Your username, name, bio, photos, captions, likes and comments are visible to other logged-in members. Photos are stored with a web link, so anyone who has the link to a photo can open it. Your email address and password are not shown to other members.

4. Where it is stored

Your data is stored with our service providers: Supabase (database, login and photo storage) and GitHub (website hosting). They process data on our behalf and use encryption in transit and at rest.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete your profile, posts and comments within a reasonable time, except where the law requires us to keep something.

6. Your choices

  • Edit your name, username and bio in your profile.
  • Delete your own posts at any time.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your data by emailing genshare.help@gmail.com.

7. Children

Genshare is only for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that a younger person has an account, we will delete it.

8. Security

We use passwords that are stored in protected form, access rules on our database, and encrypted connections. No service is perfectly secure, so use a strong password and tell us about any problem.

9. Contact and complaints

Deepak Ahirwar, genshare.help@gmail.com. We may update this policy and will post the new date here.
